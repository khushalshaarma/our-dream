## 🌸 The 3 Core Sections (Nothing Extra, Maximum Impact)

1. **🎬 The 3D Movie Checklist Dashboard**:
   - Immediately opens when you unlock with the secret code: **`08/08/2026`**.
   - **Editable Movie List**: Easily add movies of your choice with title, year, and cozy notes.
   - **Inline Editing**: Click any title or notes to edit in place.
   - **Heart Checkbox**: Tick the heart to mark a movie as watched with an elastic 3D pop and star burst!
   - **Date Visible on the Side**: The moment you tick a movie, **the date watched is displayed prominently on the side** (e.g. `♡ Watched: Nov 16, 2026`). Click the date to adjust if needed!
   - Filter pills: `All Movies`, `To Watch (Unchecked)`, `Watched (Checked ♡)`.
2. **📅 The Midnight Calendar**:
   - 3D diary tracking the dates you completed your movie dates.
   - Pinned to `Asia/Kolkata` (IST) timezone so late-night movie dates never drift.
   - Special anniversary marker on **August 8** (`Lore Day`).
3. **📸 The Polaroid Corner**:
   - 3D floating photo memory wall with washi tape stickers.
   - Upload real pictures from movie nights with captions and dates.
   - Click to enlarge and download keepsakes!
